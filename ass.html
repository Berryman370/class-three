INGREDIENTS FOR JOLLOF RICE

  • Tomato paste
  • Fresh tomato
  • onions
  • Vegetable oil
  • Seasoning cubes
  • Salt
  • Curry powder
  • Bay leaves
  • Thyme
  • Butter

STEPS IN COOKING JOLLOF RICE

Step 1
Add the fried stew to the pot.
Step 2
Add 2-3 cups of water to the rice.
Step 3
Add seasoning,currry,thyme,bay leaves and salt.
Step 4
Add the washed rice and mix well.
Step 5
Cover and cook on a low heat for 30 minutes.
Step 6
Add a littile water if the rice is hard.
Step 7
When cooked, stir gently and serve.
